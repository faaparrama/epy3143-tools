Concept Selection

Part 1 — Your Instructional Plan

2 pts · Do this before AI

Complete this section on your own before opening any chatbot.

What experience, question, or contradiction will force students to realize their current schema won't work? Avoid just telling or re-explaining.

Think about what students can do with support but not alone. What does the scaffold look like, and when do you pull it back?

Part 2 — AI Response

Use ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, etc.

Use this prompt (swap in your concept and grade):

"Explain [your concept] for a student in grade [your grade] and give me two scaffolds. Don't just simplify vocabulary; actually adjust the type of explanation to match how this age/grade typically thinks."

This is the one field that should be pasted.

Part 3 — Your Evaluation

4 pts

Now compare the AI's response against what you would have done.

Part 4 — Peer Feedback

4 pts

Review a classmate's AI response (Part 2). Imagine you are a student at that age. How would you improve your classmate's AI response based on what you learned this week?
